Our Story

Born in OKC. Built on Philippians 4:13.

4:13 Run Club started right here in Oklahoma City with one simple idea: lace up, show up, and do it together. Every run is a chance to build community, grow stronger, and walk out our faith side by side — one mile at a time.

“To remind people that they're capable of more than they think. Similar to life, running shows us that even when the body wants to quit, there's strength to keep going — and through Christ, we never run alone.”

Community

We run in a pack, not alone. Every pace and every person is welcome at the back, the front, and everywhere between.

Strength

Physical and spiritual. We train the body, lean on the Spirit, and remind each other we can do all things.

Fellowship

Coffee after. Conversation always. The friendships built here tend to outlast the mile.

"I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."

Philippians 4:13
